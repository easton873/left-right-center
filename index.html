🎲 Left, Right, Center

The classic dice game — you versus the computer.

How to play
  • Everyone starts with 3 tokens. Turns go clockwise.
  • On your turn, roll one die per token you hold (max 3).
  • L: pass a token to the player on your left.
  • R: pass a token to the player on your right.
  • C: put a token in the center (out of the game).
  • Dot: keep the token.
  • Players with no tokens skip their turn, but can get tokens passed back to them.
  • The last player holding tokens wins!
Center pot: 0
Clockwise seating: L passes to the next player clockwise, R to the previous one.
